The Method

How Picks Work

  1. I

    Soft Quality Floor

    Books whose early readers disliked them are set down before they ever reach your shelf. The floor is soft by design — one weak run of reviews and a title quietly disappears.

  2. II

    Fresh Read

    A FRESH READ badge marks a new book that is not yet proven — it has no rating sample. It is flagged openly, so you know you are reading it on trust. The tracker decides if it returns.

  3. III

    Fit Score

    Every pick is ranked by fit — the genres you read, the tropes you mark on the tracker, and the spice band you prefer. Your tracker states feed next month's ranking.

  4. IV

    Trad Published Only

    Recommendations come from traditional publishers only. Indie and self-published titles never appear on the shelf.

  5. V

    M/F Romance Only

    The Shelf carries men-and-women romance only. No M/M or F/F titles are included.
